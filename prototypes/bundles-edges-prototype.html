What should Bundles and Page Edges look like?

One Spread: → / ← (or click the right / left half). Jump 10 pages: Shift + → / ←. Start / end: Home / End. Or use the jump buttons. [ and ] switch the Page Edges style.
To decide:
  1. Which Page Edges style (bottom bar).
  2. How thick Page Edges are, for short and long books (try Book length).
  3. How thick a Bundle is: per page, and at most.
  4. How a Bundle lifts and lands, and when the Page Edges change.
  5. Page Edges in Single Page view: decided, only the unread pages. Read pages just disappear.
