PROTOTYPE - Mixed Sizes

Mixed Page Size Transitions

Turn: →/PageDown/Space or click right. Back: ←/PageUp or click left. Hold to turn fast.
Testing four ways the page surroundings transition when sizes differ:
  • A · Settle crossfade: old screen stays, crossfades to new at end
  • B · Morph: outline smoothly changes from old to new
  • C · Album: all pages on same-size sheets (largest)
  • D · Two beats: turn with old outline, then quick slide to new
